Who we are

World of Wonders Studio is run by Shubha M in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, as part of the OurSchoolHouse (OSH) family of education and content brands. Our YouTube channels are World of Wonders Studio, OurSchoolHouse, QUAD by OSH (QUAD Academy) and Jagruth Bharath. Contact: info@ourschoolhouse.in.

OSH Studio Publisher and YouTube API Services

OSH Studio Publisher is a private tool used only by us to publish videos on our own four YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and no outside user can sign in to it. It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload our own videos as private and schedule their publish time;
  • set thumbnails, add caption files and add videos to playlists;
  • update titles, descriptions and tags of our own videos;
  • read our own channels' video lists to check what is already scheduled.

What data it accesses. Only data of our own channels: channel ID and title, our video IDs, titles, descriptions, tags, privacy and schedule settings, thumbnails, captions and playlists. It does not access viewers' personal data, comments, subscribers' identities or any other user's account.

How we sign in. Each channel owner signs in once through Google's own OAuth 2.0 consent screen. The resulting access tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a folder that only the owner can open. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party.

Storage and deletion. We keep video metadata copies (upload kits and logs) only as long as needed to manage our channels, and refresh any data obtained from the API at least every 30 days or delete it. When a channel is disconnected we delete its stored token and API data within 7 days.

Revoking access. Anyone who has granted access can revoke it at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

This website

  • Videos. Pages embed our YouTube videos with the YouTube player. YouTube may set its own cookies and collect data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Games and language choice. We store small settings in your browser (local storage): your language, which games you have unlocked, and whether you joined our list. They stay on your device; we cannot see them. Clear your browser data to remove them.
  • Join form. If a parent chooses to join our updates list, we collect the name, email and WhatsApp/phone number entered, with consent, through a Google Form. We use them only to send World of Wonders and OSH updates and never sell or share them. To be removed, write to info@ourschoolhouse.in and we delete the entry within 30 days.
  • Sign in with Google or Facebook. If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook" when joining, we receive only your name and email address from that account. We never receive your password, contacts or posts, and we do not post anything on your behalf. We use the name and email only as described for the join form above. You can remove our access any time in your Google Account (Security › Third-party connections) or Facebook (Settings › Apps and websites), and to have your data deleted, write to info@ourschoolhouse.in; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Visit counts. We use a simple page-hit counter (hits.sh) that counts visits without identifying you.
  • Children. Our games collect no personal data from children, and ads are never personalised. The join form is meant for parents and guardians only.

Advertising

To keep the site free, some pages may show ads served by Google AdSense, and our games may show a short ad between rounds (never during play). Because our audience is families and children, ads on this site are set to be non-personalised: Google does not use your browsing history or interests to choose them, and they are not targeted at children. Google may still use cookies or similar technology to count ads, limit how often an ad repeats and prevent fraud. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

When our games are played on other game websites (such as CrazyGames, GameDistribution or itch.io), ads there are shown and controlled by those websites under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We do not set advertising cookies ourselves. YouTube (for videos) and Google (for ads, when shown) may set their own cookies as described above. Visitors in regions that require it are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before any ad cookies are used.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by writing to info@ourschoolhouse.in. We reply within 30 days.
