Questions

What is World of Wonders Studio?

World of Wonders Studio makes free animated stories, games, devotional songs and daily thoughts on Bharat's festivals, epics, scriptures and Vedic Maths, for children and families. Watch the story, play the game, learn together. It is a sister site of OurSchoolHouse and QUAD Academy.

Is it free?

Yes. All the games, stories, songs and videos on this site and on our YouTube channel are free. Printable workbooks and story books are coming soon.

Which languages are available?

English, Hindi and Kannada. The Dasara and Navratri videos are also in Telugu.

What Deepavali games are there?

Diya ki Bharat Yatra is an 8-episode cartoon series in which Diya, a little clay lamp, travels across India and finds each region's own Deepavali story. Every episode has a game: Road to Ayodhya, Samudra Manthan, Lagori Strike, Satyabhama's Arrow, Lakshmi's Cowries, Catch Every Drop, Bali's Promise and Yamuna's Thali, plus the Light Up Deepavali finale where you light 24 diyas. Each game opens after you watch its story video.

What Dasara games are there?

A game for each day of Navratri, each with its own way of playing and a short story of that day's Devi: Navadurga Match (a sliding picture puzzle of the nine Devis), Climb the Himalaya (Day 1, Maa Shailaputri), Japa Mala Beads (Day 2, Maa Brahmacharini) and Ring the Bell (Day 3, Maa Chandraghanta). More days are coming. Each game opens after you watch its Navratri video.

What is Vedic Maths Gurukul Games?

Free Vedic Maths practice games for Classes 6–10. Six tricks for fast mental maths, each a game adventure with Ganak, Meera, Acharya ji and Chintu that starts with a short lesson from Acharya ji. Progress is saved on your device.

What is Scam Alert (Jagruth Bharath)?

Games and stories built from real Indian scam cases. Mauna the detective owl explains how Mr. Promise, a scammer jackal, tricks people. Play Spot the Scam, Mr. Promise is calling, and the Family Safety Check, in English, Hindi and Kannada. If you are a victim of cyber fraud in India, call the national helpline 1930.

What is A Thought a Day?

One story, one shloka and one small step to apply it, every day, in English, Hindi and Kannada.

Where can I hear your songs?

The Songs section on our home page has our bhajans and festival songs for Deepavali and Lakshmi Puja, Kannada Rajyotsava, Navratri and Dasara, and saints' and wisdom songs. They are also on our YouTube channel.

How do I get new episodes?

Subscribe to World of Wonders Studio on YouTube, follow our WhatsApp channel, or join the family with the sign-up box at the bottom of any page.
