Privacy in Diya's Bharat Yatra: Match Journey

World of Wonders Studio · last updated 12 November 2026

Diya's Bharat Yatra is a tile-matching game for children and families. We built it so that it does not collect any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your own device.

What the game keeps, and where

  • The player's first name and picture choice, typed in by the player so Diya can greet them and put their name on postcards and certificates.
  • Game progress: levels, stars, coins, stickers, language and sound settings.

These are saved only in the browser or app storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's or browser's data removes them completely.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No advertising of any kind, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or behavioural monitoring.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • No chat or contact with other players.

Sharing and links

Children can make postcards, certificates and coloured pictures. Saving one only saves the picture to your device. Sharing a picture, sending an invite on WhatsApp, or opening a state tourism website first asks a grown-up to answer a simple sum. Anything shared goes through your device's own share options; the game itself sends nothing.

Pictures and facts

The folk-art pictures in the game were made by World of Wonders Studio with the help of AI image tools. The facts about places, food and crafts are written for children and checked against public sources.

Questions or requests

Write to info@ourschoolhouse.in. You can also read the World of Wonders Studio privacy policy for the rest of our website.
